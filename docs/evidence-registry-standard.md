# Verilogix Evidence Registry Standard

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundational

---

# Purpose

The Evidence Registry Standard defines how evidence is created, stored, classified, referenced, validated, and retained within Verilogix.

Evidence is the foundation of governance.

Evidence provides objective proof that work has been performed, requirements have been satisfied, and progression has been earned.

No validation may exist without evidence.

No approval may exist without evidence.

No progression may exist without evidence.

---

# Definition

Evidence is any artefact that demonstrates compliance with a defined requirement.

Evidence may be:

* Created
* Uploaded
* Generated
* Captured
* Recorded
* Observed

Evidence serves as proof.

Evidence does not determine compliance.

Evidence supports validation.

---

# Constitutional Principle

Evidence before approval.

Evidence before progression.

Evidence shall remain subordinate to constitutional governance.

---

# Evidence Object

Every evidence record shall contain:

* Evidence ID
* Evidence Type
* Evidence Title
* Evidence Source
* Linked Project
* Linked Stage
* Owner
* Creation Date
* Status
* Retention Policy

No evidence may exist without traceability.

---

# Evidence ID

Every evidence item shall possess a unique identifier.

Format:

```text
EV-YYYY-NNNNN
```

Examples:

```text
EV-2026-00001
EV-2026-00002
EV-2026-00003
```

Requirements:

* Unique
* Immutable
* Auditable

---

# Evidence Classification

Evidence shall be classified.

Supported classifications include:

```text
Document
Image
Video
Audio
Observation
Assessment
Approval
Certificate
Form
Contract
Drawing
Report
System Record
Communication
```

Frameworks may extend classifications.

---

# Evidence Source

Evidence must declare origin.

Examples:

```text
Customer
Employee
Assessor
Auditor
Supplier
System
AI Agent
Third Party
```

Source must be recorded.

Unknown sources are prohibited.

---

# Evidence Ownership

Every evidence item shall have an owner.

Examples:

```text
Project Manager
Operations Manager
Assessor
Engineer
Guide Coordinator
```

Ownership establishes accountability.

---

# Evidence Scope

Evidence may relate to:

```text
Project
Stage
Task
Assessment
Approval
Risk
Audit
Framework
```

Scope must be declared.

---

# Evidence Status

Valid statuses include:

```text
Draft
Submitted
Under Review
Validated
Rejected
Archived
Superseded
```

Status changes must be auditable.

---

# Evidence Integrity

Evidence integrity must be preserved.

Evidence shall:

* Remain unchanged after submission
* Record revision history
* Maintain timestamps
* Preserve authorship

Tampering with evidence is prohibited.

# Evidence Validation

Evidence itself may require validation.

Validation may determine:

* Authenticity
* Completeness
* Relevance
* Accuracy
* Sufficiency

Evidence failing validation shall not support progression.

---

# Evidence Sufficiency

Evidence must be sufficient for the decision being made.

Examples:

A passport image may be sufficient for identity verification.

A passport image is not sufficient for expedition readiness.

Evidence requirements are determined by framework rules.

---

# Evidence Relationships

Evidence may be linked to:

* Projects
* Stages
* Risks
* Approvals
* Audits
* Deviations
* Findings

Relationships shall be maintained throughout the lifecycle.

---

# Evidence Retention

Evidence shall be retained according to governance requirements.

Retention periods may vary by framework.

Examples:

```text
Operational Records     3 Years
Commercial Records      7 Years
Audit Records           7 Years
Risk Records            7 Years
Assessment Records      5 Years
```

Deletion shall be controlled.

---

# Evidence Lifecycle

Every evidence item follows a lifecycle.

```text
Created
    ↓
Submitted
    ↓
Reviewed
    ↓
Validated
    ↓
Used for Governance
    ↓
Archived
```

Alternative paths:

```text
Rejected
Superseded
Withdrawn
```

---

# Evidence Audit Requirements

Every evidence item shall record:

* Evidence ID
* Creator
* Owner
* Linked Objects
* Status History
* Validation Results
* Revisions
* Access History

Evidence activity must be auditable.

---

# Evidence and Intelligence

Evidence contributes to organisational intelligence.

Historical evidence may be used for:

* Lessons Learned
* Trend Analysis
* Performance Evaluation
* Framework Improvement

Evidence becomes knowledge when analysed.

---

# Governance Effect

Evidence supports:

* Validation
* Approval
* Audit
* Investigation
* Continuous Improvement

Without evidence, governance becomes opinion.

With evidence, governance becomes objective.

---

# Foundational Principle

Evidence is not documentation.

Evidence is proof.

The purpose of evidence is to provide objective support for governance decisions and progression authority within Verilogix.
