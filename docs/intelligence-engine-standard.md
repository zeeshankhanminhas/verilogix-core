# Verilogix Intelligence Engine Standard

**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Foundational

---

# Purpose

The Intelligence Engine defines how Verilogix captures, organises, analyses, and applies knowledge generated through governed execution.

The purpose of the Intelligence Engine is to ensure that experience becomes institutional knowledge.

The system must become more effective over time.

Every project, stage, validation, approval, audit, assessment, deviation, and outcome shall contribute to organisational intelligence.

---

# Constitutional Principle

Knowledge shall not remain dependent upon memory.

Experience shall be captured.

Learning shall be retained.

Improvement shall be systematic.

---

# Definition

Intelligence is structured knowledge derived from evidence, validation, approvals, audits, observations, outcomes, and lessons learned.

Intelligence transforms:

```text
Data
    ↓
Information
    ↓
Knowledge
    ↓
Intelligence
    ↓
Improvement
```

The Intelligence Engine governs this transformation.

---

# Intelligence Object

Every intelligence record shall contain:

* Intelligence ID
* Intelligence Type
* Intelligence Source
* Intelligence Category
* Observation
* Impact
* Recommendation
* Status
* Date Created

No intelligence may exist without traceability.

---

# Intelligence ID

Format:

```text
INT-YYYY-NNNNN
```

Examples:

```text
INT-2026-00001
INT-2026-00002
INT-2026-00003
```

Requirements:

* Unique
* Immutable
* Auditable

# Intelligence Capture

Intelligence may be captured through:

* Manual entry
* Structured reviews
* Audits
* Surveys
* Incident investigations
* AI-assisted analysis
* Automated system observations

Capture mechanisms must be auditable.

---

# Observation

Every intelligence record shall include an observation.

Examples:

```text
Travellers consistently rated Hotel A higher than Hotel B.

Technical reviews identified recurring drawing revision errors.

Expeditions scheduled in October experienced fewer weather delays.
```

Observations shall be factual.

---

# Impact Assessment

Every intelligence item shall define impact.

Impact categories:

```text
Low
Medium
High
Critical
```

Impact determines prioritisation.

---

# Recommendation

Every intelligence item should contain a recommendation.

Examples:

```text
Replace Hotel B with Hotel A.

Update technical review checklist.

Introduce additional risk control.
```

Recommendations support improvement.

---

# Intelligence Status

Valid statuses include:

```text
Captured
Under Review
Accepted
Rejected
Implemented
Archived
```

Status changes must be auditable.

---

# Lessons Learned

Lessons learned are a specialised form of intelligence.

Every completed project should contribute lessons learned.

Examples:

```text
What worked?

What failed?

What should change?

What should remain?
```

Lessons learned become framework intelligence.

---

# Trend Analysis

The Intelligence Engine shall support trend identification.

Examples:

* Recurring failures
* Repeated risks
* Customer satisfaction trends
* Supplier performance trends
* Approval bottlenecks

Patterns are more valuable than isolated events.

---

Intelligence shall influence governance.

* New dependencies
* New approval requirements
* New evidence requirements
* New stages
* Updated framework standards

The framework must evolve.

# AI-Assisted Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence may assist by:

* Detecting anomalies
* Generating recommendations

* Discovering trends
